Task 1: Business Constraints

This section details the two fundamental constraints that defined the entire modeling strategy: the absence of ground truth labels and the strict requirement for regulatory compliance.

🚫 No Default Labels

**Challenge:** Traditional supervised learning is impossible. The solution requires engineering a **proxy target variable** using customer behavior.

Potential Risks: Misclassification (behavioral disengagement ≠ default) and Regulatory concerns.

⚖️ Basel II Interpretability

**Mandate:** Models must be transparent and auditable. Complex, non-linear models risk non-compliance.

Approach: Use Logistic Regression with WoE for guaranteed monotonic transformations and explainable predictions.

📊 Data Insights (Task 2 & 3)

  • Amounts are skewed, requiring `RobustScaler`.
  • High-Cardinality features mandate WoE encoding.
  • Weak raw correlations required **RFM** and aggregate features.

Task 4: Proxy Target Engineering (RFM Clustering)

To enable supervised learning, K-Means clustering ($k=3$) was applied to RFM metrics (Recency, Frequency, Monetary). The cluster representing the least active customers was labeled **High-Risk (1)**.
